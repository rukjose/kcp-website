Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Kigali Citizens Polyclinic, our medical services, location, and bookings.

Where is Kigali Citizens Polyclinic located?

Kigali Citizens Polyclinic is located at KG 176 St, 4, Amahoro Village, Rukiri 2 Cell, Remera Sector, Gasabo District, Kigali, Rwanda. We are easily accessible from across Kigali and located close to Amahoro Stadium.

What medical services and specialties are offered?

We provide comprehensive outpatient and specialized healthcare across 8 major departments: Internal Medicine, Paediatrics, Dental Surgery, Ophthalmology (Eye Care), Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT), Obstetrics & Gynecology (OB-GYN), General Practice, and Physiotherapy.

How can I book an appointment with a doctor?

You can book an appointment online anytime through our website by clicking any "Book Your Appointment" button, or by calling our clinic reception line at 0789 923 130.

Do you accept walk-in patients or only bookings?

We gladly welcome both walk-in patients and booked appointments. However, booking in advance online or via phone is recommended to minimize waiting time and guarantee prompt consultation with your preferred specialist.

What are the operating hours of Kigali Citizens Polyclinic?

Kigali Citizens Polyclinic operates from Monday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with dedicated medical professionals available to provide timely and attentive care for you and your family.
